JUDGE MODEL: N/A — news_cycle is deterministic-floor by design (per CM Signal PRD §5.1)

PURPOSE
News-cycle wires are NOT judged for editorial significance. They publish on
coverage, not selection. This file documents the MECHANICAL pipeline that produces
them so consumers can audit the deterministic logic.

PIPELINE
1. Once daily at 04:00 ET:
   - Call Perplexity for top ~5 news stories of the prior 24h (with citations)
   - Filter to institutionally-relevant topics (macro, geopolitics, regulation,
     major corporate events)

2. For each story:
   - Extract entities (people, companies, countries, instruments)
   - Mechanically match entities to active PM markets (entity_slug_match)
   - If 1+ markets match, publish a wire item

3. No LLM judge gate. Coverage of top news IS the product.

ENTITY MATCHING
entity_slug_match is implemented as a token-overlap score between:
  - Story entity tokens (e.g., "Fed", "Powell", "rate cut")
  - PM market question tokens
Threshold: 2+ overlapping non-stopword tokens.

OUTPUT
There is no judge_verdict for news_cycle wires. The judge_engine field in the
wire record reads "none — deterministic news-cycle scan (04:00 ET)".
